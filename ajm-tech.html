Got Questions?
Frequently Asked Questions
How long does a typical phone repair take?

Most screen replacements and battery swaps are done within 1–3 hours. More complex motherboard repairs may take 24–48 hours. We always give you an honest timeline upfront.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. All hardware repairs carry a 30-day warranty on parts and labour. If the same issue recurs within that period, we fix it at no extra cost.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope. A basic landing page starts from UGX 500,000, while full e-commerce platforms vary. Contact us for a free custom quote tailored to your needs.

Can you design a logo and brand identity from scratch?

Absolutely. Our graphics team handles full branding projects — logo, colours, typography, business cards, letterheads, and brand style guides.

Do you offer IT support contracts for businesses?

Yes. We offer monthly and annual support contracts that include scheduled maintenance, remote helpdesk, and on-site visits. Great for SMEs and schools.

Where are you located?

We're based in Kampala, Uganda. We also offer remote services for software, design, and development work — so clients anywhere in Uganda (and beyond) can work with us.
