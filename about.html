How It All Started
From a Single Repair
Bench to a Full Studio

AJM-TECH started in 2019 in a small workshop in Kampala. Our founder, a passionate engineer with a decade of experience in device repair and software, noticed a glaring gap: Ugandans were paying premium prices for mediocre tech services — or travelling far for basic repairs.

We began with phone and laptop repairs, quickly building a reputation for honesty, speed, and quality work. Word spread. Clients began asking for websites. Then logos. Then mobile apps. With each new request, we grew the team and the services.

Today, AJM-TECH is a full-spectrum technology company — a place where a market vendor can get their phone screen fixed, and a startup can build their entire digital identity, all under one roof, all in Kampala.

Our Journey
🔧
2019 — The Beginning
First Repair Bay Opens
Started with device repairs — phones, laptops, tablets — out of a workshop in central Kampala. First month: 40 repairs. Word-of-mouth spread fast.
🎨
2020 — Expanding Services
Graphics & Web Design Added
Client demand pushed us into branding and web development. Hired our first designer and launched the digital services arm.
📱
2021 — Going Mobile
App Development Division
Launched our mobile app development team. First major project: a boda-boda dispatch app now used by 500+ daily riders.
🌐
2023 — IT Infrastructure
Full IT & CCTV Services
Added enterprise IT support, structured cabling, and CCTV installation. Signed first corporate contract with a Kampala logistics firm.
🚀
2025–26 — Today
300+ Projects & Growing
12-person team, new expanded service bay, and plans to open a second branch. Still the same mission: world-class tech, Ugandan prices.
