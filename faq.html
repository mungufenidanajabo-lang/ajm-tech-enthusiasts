🔧 Tech Repairs
How long does a typical phone repair take?

Most screen replacements and battery swaps are done within 1–3 hours. More complex motherboard repairs may take 24–48 hours. We always give you an honest timeline upfront before we begin any work.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. All hardware repairs carry a 30-day warranty on parts and labour. If the same issue recurs within that period, we fix it at no extra cost.

What devices do you repair?

We repair smartphones (Android & iPhone), laptops, MacBooks, tablets, desktop computers, and peripherals. If you're unsure whether we can fix your device, just call or WhatsApp us and we'll advise you immediately.

Do you use genuine parts?

We use genuine or high-quality OEM-equivalent parts for all repairs. We'll always inform you of the part quality and give you options where alternatives exist — so you decide what works best for your budget.

Can you recover data from a damaged device?

In many cases yes. We offer data recovery services for phones, laptops, and hard drives. Success depends on the extent of the damage. We'll give you an honest assessment before charging anything.

💻 Web Development & Design
How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on scope. A basic landing page starts from UGX 500,000, while full e-commerce platforms vary. Contact us for a free custom quote tailored to your needs and budget.

Can you design a logo and brand identity from scratch?

Absolutely. Our graphics team handles full branding projects — logo, colours, typography, business cards, letterheads, and full brand style guides. We deliver multiple concepts for you to choose from.

How long does a website project take?

A simple landing page can be ready in 3–5 business days. A full multi-page business site typically takes 1–3 weeks. E-commerce and web app projects are scoped individually. We commit to clear timelines upfront.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes — every website we build is fully responsive and tested across phones, tablets, and desktops. Mobile performance is a core part of our development process, not an afterthought.

Do you offer website hosting and maintenance?

Yes. We offer affordable hosting packages and optional monthly maintenance plans that cover security updates, backups, content changes, and performance monitoring.

Can you build a mobile app for my business?

Yes. We build native and cross-platform apps for Android and iOS using Flutter and React Native. We can take your project from concept and wireframes all the way to app store submission and post-launch support.

🖥️ IT Support & CCTV
Do you offer IT support contracts for businesses?

Yes. We offer monthly and annual support contracts that include scheduled maintenance, remote helpdesk, and on-site visits. Great for SMEs, NGOs, and schools that need reliable ongoing technical support.

Can you set up a CCTV system for my office or home?

Yes. We design, supply, and install CCTV systems for homes, offices, schools, and warehouses. We also configure remote viewing so you can monitor your premises from your phone anywhere in the world.

Do you handle network setup and server installation?

Yes. We handle structured cabling, router and switch configuration, Wi-Fi access point installation, and server setup. We've equipped entire school computer labs and corporate offices from scratch.

What's included in your remote IT support?

Remote support covers software troubleshooting, virus/malware removal, configuration issues, and general helpdesk queries. For hardware issues, we schedule an on-site visit as part of your support contract.

🛒 Tech Sales
Do you sell new and refurbished devices?

Yes. We stock both brand new devices and certified refurbished phones, laptops, and accessories. All refurbished items are thoroughly tested and come with a warranty. We'll always tell you exactly what you're buying.

Can I get ink and toner cartridges from you?

Yes. We stock a wide range of ink and toner cartridges for popular printer brands. We also sell printers, scanners, keyboards, mice, cables, and general computer accessories.

Do you offer bulk purchasing for businesses or schools?

Absolutely. We offer competitive pricing for bulk orders of devices, accessories, and office tech. Contact us with your requirements and we'll put together a tailored quote and delivery plan.

💰 Pricing & Payment
Do you charge for diagnostics?

No. We offer free diagnostics for all devices. We'll tell you exactly what's wrong and what it will cost to fix — with no obligation to proceed. You only pay when you approve the repair.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash (UGX), Mobile Money (MTN & Airtel), and bank transfers. For large projects, we work on a milestone payment structure — typically 50% upfront and 50% on completion.

Do you offer quotes before starting a project?

Always. We never begin work without providing a clear, itemised quote first. For repairs, the quote is free. For design and development projects, we provide a detailed proposal outlining scope, timeline, and cost.

📍 General
Where are you located?

We're based in Kampala, Uganda. We also offer remote services for software, design, and development work — so clients anywhere in Uganda (and beyond) can work with us without needing to visit in person.

What are your working hours?

We're open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 7pm. For urgent repairs or after-hours support, reach us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to assist or schedule an emergency visit.

Can I reach you online if I'm outside Kampala?

Yes. For web development, app development, graphics design, and remote IT support, we work with clients across Uganda and internationally. All communication, project updates, and file sharing can be handled fully online.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help. Reach us by phone, WhatsApp, or email — we'll get back to you fast.
