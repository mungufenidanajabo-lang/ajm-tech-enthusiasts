🔧 Repairs 6 questions
⏱️How long does a typical repair take?+

Turnaround depends on the repair type:

  • Screen replacement — 1–2 hours
  • Battery replacement — 30–60 minutes
  • Charging port / headphone jack — 1–2 hours
  • Water damage treatment — 24–48 hours (drying + diagnostics)
  • Motherboard / logic board repair — 2–5 business days
  • Laptop screen / keyboard — 1–3 hours (parts in stock) or 1–3 days (if order needed)

We always give you an honest timeline at drop-off and will call or WhatsApp you the moment your device is ready.

🛡️Do you offer a warranty on repairs?+

Yes — all hardware repairs carry a 30-day warranty covering both parts and labour. If the exact same fault returns within that window, we fix it at no extra cost.

Note: the warranty does not cover new physical damage (drops, liquid exposure) that occurs after the repair.

✔ 30-Day Warranty Included
💧What should I do if my phone got wet?+

Do immediately:

  • Power it off — do NOT try to turn it on
  • Remove SIM card, memory card, and case
  • Gently pat dry with a soft cloth
  • Bring it to us as fast as possible — every hour matters

Do NOT: put it in rice (ineffective), use a hairdryer, charge it, or press any buttons repeatedly.

Our technicians will open it, clean the board with isopropyl alcohol, and assess component damage. The sooner you come in, the better the recovery odds.

🔩Do you use genuine / original parts?+

We use the best available parts for every job and are transparent about it:

  • OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) parts — used wherever available, especially for Apple and Samsung screens
  • High-quality aftermarket — used when OEM is unavailable or when the client prefers a lower cost option; always disclosed upfront

We will never install a cheap knock-off part without telling you. You choose what goes into your device.

📊Can you recover data from a damaged device?+

In many cases, yes. We offer data recovery services for:

  • Phones with broken screens (data extracted to a new phone or drive)
  • Water-damaged devices
  • Laptops with failed hard drives or SSDs
  • Devices that won't power on

Success rate depends on the extent of damage. We run a free initial diagnostic and give you an honest assessment before you commit to anything.

💰How much does a repair cost?+

Pricing varies by device and fault. Rough guides:

  • Phone screen (Samsung/Tecno/Infinix) — UGX 40,000 – 180,000
  • iPhone screen — UGX 150,000 – 450,000
  • Laptop screen — UGX 120,000 – 350,000
  • Battery (phone) — UGX 35,000 – 85,000
  • Charging port — UGX 25,000 – 70,000

All quotes are given free of charge before any work begins. Walk in or WhatsApp us a description of the fault.

🛒 Tech Sales 4 questions
📦What products do you sell?+

We stock a wide range of technology products, including:

  • Smartphones (Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, iPhone, Xiaomi)
  • Laptops and desktops
  • Accessories: chargers, cables, power banks, earphones, cases

Stock varies — WhatsApp us to confirm availability on a specific item.

🔄Do you sell refurbished devices?+

Yes. We sell certified refurbished phones and laptops that have been inspected, repaired where necessary, and tested by our technicians. Every refurbished device is clearly labelled and comes with a 14-day exchange warranty.

Refurbished devices are a great way to get quality hardware at 30–50% below new prices.

🚚Can you deliver products to me?+

Yes — we offer delivery within Kampala and its suburbs. Delivery fees depend on your location. For orders outside Kampala, we work with trusted couriers.

Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange delivery. Payment on delivery is available for verified repeat clients; new orders require 50% upfront.

↩️What is your return / exchange policy?+

New products: 7-day exchange for manufacturing defects. Items must be in original, unactivated condition with all accessories.

Refurbished products: 14-day exchange for any faults present at the time of sale.

We do not accept returns for change of mind once a device has been activated or used. All sales of accessories (cables, cases, earphones) are final once opened.

📁What file formats will I receive?+

🛠️What technologies do you build with?+

We choose the right tool for the right job. Our core stack includes:

  • Backend: Node.js, Laravel (PHP), Python (Django/Flask)

Absolutely — every website we build is mobile-first by default. Given that 80%+ of Uganda's internet traffic comes from smartphones, we don't treat mobile as an afterthought.

We test across multiple real devices and screen sizes before delivery. We also optimise for slow/3G connections common in parts of Uganda.

✔ Mobile-First, Always

📱 Mobile App Development 4 questions
🤔Android, iOS, or both — which do you build?+

We build for both, and typically recommend cross-platform development using Flutter or React Native — one codebase that runs natively on both Android and iOS. This cuts cost and time significantly without sacrificing performance.

For projects that specifically need native iOS (Swift) or native Android (Kotlin), we handle those too.

📊How long does it take to build an app?+
  • Simple MVP (basic features) — 6–10 weeks
  • Mid-complexity app — 3–5 months
  • Complex app (multi-role, real-time, payments) — 5–9 months

We recommend starting with an MVP to validate your idea quickly, then adding features in subsequent phases.

🏪Will you publish the app to the Play Store / App Store?+

Yes — app store submission is included in all our mobile app packages. We handle:

  • Google Play Store listing (screenshots, description, keywords)
  • Apple App Store submission and review management
  • All required assets (icons, splash screens)

You will need a Google Play developer account (one-time $25 fee) and/or an Apple Developer account ($99/year), both registered in your name.

🔄Do you provide post-launch support?+

All app projects come with 60 days of free post-launch support covering bug fixes and minor adjustments. After that, we offer flexible monthly maintenance retainers for:

  • OS update compatibility (Android/iOS new versions)
  • Bug fixes and performance improvements
  • Feature additions and updates
  • Server/backend monitoring
